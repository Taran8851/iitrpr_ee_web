Introduction

Ashwani Sharma (Member, IEEE) received the master’s degree in technology and communication systems from ETSIT, Technical University of Madrid (UPM), Madrid, Spain, in 2013, and a Ph.D. degree from the University of Deusto, Bilbao, Spain, in 2015. He has been working as an Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, Rupnagar, India, since July 2018. He was a Visiting Training Fellow at the University of Kent, Canterbury, U.K., from May 2014 to August 2014, and a Junior Research Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology, New Delhi, India, from 2010 to 2011. His research works have been published in various international journals and conferences such as IEEE TRANSACTIONS AND LETTERS, IET journals, and Wiley letters. His current research interests include exploiting field-forming techniques in antenna design for wireless power transmission, the IoT, and 5G/6G, and Drone antennas and communications, and AI/ML Assisted communications systems.

Brief Research Profile

Dr. Ashwani Sharma specializes primarily in Antenna and Microwave Engineering with a focus on Wireless power and data transfer, RF Energy Harvesting, Antenna design for IoT and 5G/B5G, Near-field forming and its applications, Static and Dynamic Wireless Charging Solutions for Drones/Electric Vehicles. Additionally, he has supervised several PhD, Master's, and undergraduate students.
