Introduction

Pardeep Duhan received his Ph.D. in electrical engineering from IIT Bombay, Mumbai, India, in 2017. He was with GlobalFoundries, Singapore, as a Senior Engineer, Process Integration, and technical staff in Fraunhofer IPMS-CNT, Dresden, Germany. In 2020, he joined the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Ropar, Rupnagar, India, as an Assistant Professor. His current research interests include nanoscale device physics, ferroelectric memory devices, thin-film transistors, CMOS technology development, and the reliability of semiconductor devices.
