Introduction

Dr. Santosh Kumar Vipparthi, a distinguished member of IEEE, brings over 12 years of experience spanning academia and industry. He is presently serving as an Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar (IIT Ropar), India. Prior to this, he held positions at the Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Indian Institute of Technology, Guwahati (IIT Guwahati) and Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT), Jaipur. Dr. Vipparthi earned his Ph.D. and MTech degrees with Honors from the Indian Institute of Technology Varanasi (IIT Varanasi), BHU, India. His research interests include computer vision, affective computing, deep learning, among others. He is actively involved in various sponsored and consultancy projects in the field of computer vision from both public and private sectors.

Brief Research Profile

Dr. Vipparthi specializes primarily in computer vision and deep learning techniques with a focus on all-weather surveillance, underwater exploration, and human emotion recognition. Additionally, he has supervised four PhD students and fifteen master's students.
