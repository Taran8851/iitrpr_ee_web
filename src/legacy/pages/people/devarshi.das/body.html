Introduction

I am an Assistant Professor in Electrical Engineering and an associate faculty at the School of AI and Data Engineering, IIT Ropar. I lead the Integrated System Design Lab (ISDL), focusing on analog, mixed-signal, and RF IC design for computing and sensing. I am a senior IEEE member and part of the AMS/RF committee of the DevSET program. I am an avid reader. Before joining academia, I was a design engineer at the GE Technology Centre (2010–2012) and a lead research engineer in Siemens Corporate R&D. (2017–2018).
