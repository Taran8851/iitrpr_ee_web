C. C. Reddy

Professor and Head of the Department
PhD in Electrical Engineering from (Indian Institute of Science) IISc Bangalore, India ME(Master of Engineering) in Electrical Engineering from (Indian Institute of Science) IISc Bangalore, India BE from University College of Engineering, Hyderabad
Research Interests : The main focus of research is on nanodielectrics for power applications. In addition, ageing, life estimation, power equipment modelling design and development research is being carried out. Latest research on spacecharges in modern ultra resistive dielectrics, external current measurement upto fempto ampere level, DC & AC breakdown tests, measurement of capacitance, loss tangent and characterization of dielectric materials is being promoted in this laboratory. TEN Ph.D. students already defended his thesis. Currently, Twelve research students are working for their thesis in this lab and are at different stages of their research program.
